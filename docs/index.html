Dino NES

The Chrome dino game as an NES ROM, running in the browser.

EnterStart a game
Space X ↑A: jump. Hold for a higher jump, let go early for a short hop. Restarts after a crash.
Z ShiftB: jetpack, in the air, while the fuel lasts. Fuel refills on the ground.
SnakesBite from the side. Land on one to bounce off it for +50.

A gamepad works too.
